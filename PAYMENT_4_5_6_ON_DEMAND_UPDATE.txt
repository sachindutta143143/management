PAYMENT 4, 5, 6 - ON-DEMAND DISPLAY

Credit / Supply Register now shows Payment 1, 2, and 3 by default.
Payment 4, 5, and 6 columns and their dashboard summary cards are hidden initially.
Use the '+ More Payments (4-6)' button to show them in one click.
Use 'Hide Payments (4-6)' to collapse them again.

The underlying Payment 4-6 fields, calculations, database payload, WhatsApp details,
and Excel import/export columns remain available. This is a display-only change;
no existing payment values or database records are removed or reset.
