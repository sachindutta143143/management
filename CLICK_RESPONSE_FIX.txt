MULTI PLAZA CLICK RESPONSE FIX

Issue: A full-screen backup reminder was automatically displayed about 1.2 seconds after page load and whenever the browser tab became visible. Its transparent dark overlay covered the whole screen (z-index 100000), so the first click could land on the overlay instead of the control underneath. This made users click a second time.

Fix: Removed the automatic blocking reminder overlay. The manual “Download All Data” backup button remains available. No business data, database tables, or records are changed by this fix.

Deploy this complete project and hard-refresh the browser (Ctrl+Shift+R). On mobile, close and reopen the installed app or refresh the page.
