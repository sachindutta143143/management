CREDIT / SUPPLY REGISTER – PHONE NUMBER + WHATSAPP SHARE

Changes:
- Added a Phone Number field immediately after Remarks.
- Added a WhatsApp Share button for every row.
- The share message includes order/bill details, customer, item, rate, quantity,
  total, all three received-payment amounts and their notes, balance/due,
  due date, and remarks.
- 10-digit Indian mobile numbers are automatically prefixed with country code 91.
- WhatsApp sharing works in view mode; entering/changing phone numbers requires edit mode.
- Phone number is included in the existing row JSON save and Excel export/import.
- Existing rows are preserved; phone is blank until entered. No records or tables are deleted.

After deployment:
1. Open Credit / Supply Register.
2. Turn Edit Mode ON.
3. Enter the customer's mobile number in Phone Number.
4. Save the register.
5. Click Share in that row to open WhatsApp with the full details prefilled.
